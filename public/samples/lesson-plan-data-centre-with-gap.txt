Lesson plan: Introduction to Data Centers (CDFA), with a deliberate gap

The same sample as lesson-plan-data-centre.txt, written for the "Introduction to Data
Centers" deck, with one section added that the deck has no slide for: "Fire suppression".
Upload it on that deck's page to see how a gap is reported to the administrator there and to
the facilitator before and during a session.

Session flow
1. Introduction and learning objectives (3 min)
   Objective: Welcome the group, introduce the session and set out what it covers.
2. The digital world and the key outputs of a data centre (4 min)
   Objective: Show how everyday digital actions depend on data centres, and what a data centre provides: computing, storage, connectivity and service delivery.
3. Basic components and the data centre as a system (5 min)
   Objective: Name the IT and facility components, and show how power, cooling, networking, monitoring and security work together as one system.
4. Fire suppression (5 min)
   Objective: Explain how gas suppression and water mist put out a fire in the data hall without damaging the equipment.
5. Why data centres exist: business operations, business drivers and data growth (9 min)
   Objective: Explain the four functions a data centre fulfils, which industries depend on them, why organisations invest in them, and how the growth of data keeps demand rising.
6. Digital infrastructure and the digital ecosystem (5 min)
   Objective: Place data centres within digital infrastructure, beside telecom networks, cloud platforms and edge facilities, and within the layered stack that connects users to applications.
7. Characteristics of modern data centres and their future (5 min)
   Objective: Describe the six characteristics a modern data centre is designed for, and the trends shaping the industry: AI, edge computing, sustainability and automation.
8. India's market, regulatory landscape and leading operators (8 min)
   Objective: Describe how India's market is growing, how the DPDP Act and data localisation rules shape demand, and who the leading operators are.
9. Session summary and review questions (2 min)
   Objective: Recap the session and check understanding with the review questions.

Learning outcomes
- Define what a data centre is and explain its purpose.
- Identify the business drivers behind investment in data centres.
- Explain what digital infrastructure is and where data centres sit within it.
- Describe the characteristics of a modern data centre.
- Recognise how data centres affect everyday life and work.
